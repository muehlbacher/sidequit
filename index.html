FAQ

How does Sidequit work?

Sidequit tracks your smoke-free time and rewards you with milestones along the way, so quitting feels like progress instead of sacrifice.

I smoked again. Is my progress lost?

A slip is part of quitting for most people. You can reset your quit date in the app at any time and start a new streak.

Do I need an account?

No. Sidequit works without an account or sign-up.

How do I delete my data?

Your data is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Is Sidequit a medical product?

No. Sidequit is a motivational tool and does not replace medical advice. If you need help quitting, talk to your doctor or a smoking cessation service.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Summary

Sidequit does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter, such as your quit date, smoking habits and progress, locally on your iPhone. This data is not sent to us or to any third party. If you use iCloud device backups, it may be included in your backup under Apple's terms.

Analytics, tracking and advertising

Sidequit does not use analytics, tracking or advertising, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

If you make an in-app purchase, payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Contacting support

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you, and delete them once your request is resolved unless we are legally required to keep them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict processing of your personal data, the right to data portability and the right to object. Since we don't store your app data, you can delete it at any time by deleting the app. You can also lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde, dsb.gv.at).

Children

Sidequit is not directed at children under 16.
